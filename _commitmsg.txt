Bring OSD to topmost layer on show

ShowInactive uses SW_SHOWNOACTIVATE which does not raise Z order, so the
panel window could sit below other topmost windows. Call SetWindowPos with
HWND_TOPMOST (SWP_NOMOVE|SWP_NOSIZE|SWP_NOACTIVATE|SWP_SHOWWINDOW) right
after showing to force it above all other windows without stealing focus.
